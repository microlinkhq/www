Microlink is built by a small team that answers its own email. There is no ticket queue and no first-line script — the people who wrote the code are the people who reply.

## Email

[hello@microlink.io](mailto:hello@microlink.io) reaches us for everything: a question about the [API](/docs/api/getting-started/overview), a quote for [Enterprise](/enterprise), a billing correction, a plan change, or an invoice that needs a VAT number on it.

Writing about a failed request? Include the `id` field from the [error response](/docs/api/basics/error-codes). It lets us replay the exact request you made instead of guessing at a reproduction.

## Bug reports

Public repositories take public issues. Open one at [github.com/microlinkhq](https://github.com/microlinkhq) against the project that misbehaved — [metascraper](https://github.com/microlinkhq/metascraper), [browserless](https://github.com/browserless/browserless), [MQL](https://github.com/microlinkhq/mql), or the API itself.

## Security

Vulnerability reports have their own channel and their own promises. Read the [security policy](/security) before disclosing, or start from the machine-readable [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt).

## Community

The [developer Slack](/community) is where integration questions get answered in the open, alongside the core engineering team. Anything discussed there is visible to other developers solving the same problem, which is usually faster than a private thread.

## Social

We post releases and infrastructure notes on [X](https://x.com/microlinkhq) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microlinkhq). Neither is a support channel; email lands faster.

## Legal entity

Microlink operates from Spain. The [terms of service](/tos), [privacy policy](/privacy) and [data processing agreement](/dpa) are governed by Spanish law and, through it, by the GDPR. Data subject requests and DPA countersignature requests go to [hello@microlink.io](mailto:hello@microlink.io).
